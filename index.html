Tetris

the harder version

How to play

In this version, every piece is a pentomino: five squares joined edge to edge. There are 18 of them, counting mirror images, and the board is two columns wider. Steer the falling pieces to build complete rows. A full row vanishes and scores points, and clearing several rows at once scores more. Every 10 rows, the pieces fall faster. The game ends when the pile reaches the top.
