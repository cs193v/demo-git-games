Gumdrop Swap

How to play

Drag a gumdrop onto the one next to it to swap them. The swap has to line up three or more of the same color in a row or a column. Those gumdrops pop, the ones above fall into the gap, and new ones drop in from the top.

Each gumdrop you pop is worth 10 points. If the falling gumdrops line up another match, that's a chain reaction, and each link in the chain is worth more than the last.

Score as many points as you can in 60 seconds!
